# ORCA visual identity
Selected direction: a single orca forming a circular O with a cyan sweep. The simpler silhouette is preferred for small app icons. Three cyan dots and a three-orca Pod are retained as explorations, not active identities.

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. These are raster artwork, not manually traced vectors. No trademark clearance is implied. Repository licensing remains undecided.

## Selected generation prompt
Original ORCA app logo variant: a bold black and white orca curves into a near circular letter O, distinctive sculptural negative space, expressive dorsal fin, tiny white eye patch, single restrained cyan accent, ivory rounded-square tile on transparent outer background, flat vector-like minimal design readable at 48px. No text, no shadows, no gradients, no shortcut arrow. One icon only. Different composition from a generic leaping whale.

## Pod exploration prompt
Original ORCA app logo variant: three minimal black and white orcas forming a balanced triangular swimming pod around a small cyan center, conveys collaboration and adaptation, generous negative space, premium flat vector-like app icon, ivory rounded square tile on transparent outer background, recognizable at small sizes. No text, no gradients, no shadow, no shortcut arrow. One icon only.

## Dots exploration prompt
Original compact ORCA app icon: a distinct black-and-white orca curving into an open circular O, white eye patch and three subtle cyan dots suggesting collaborating pods; ivory rounded tile with transparent outer background; simple flat shapes readable at 48px; no text, shortcut arrow, gradients or copied DeepSeek logo.
